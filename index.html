Frequently Asked Questions

What is a thematic investment pool?
A thematic pool groups capital around an industry thesis — like "AI Startups" or "Green Energy Q4." You invest in the theme, not a specific company. After the funding window, startups pitch and investors vote to allocate pool funds to the most promising teams.
How does voting power work?
1 token contributed = 1 vote unit. You can allocate votes across up to 3 startups per pool. You can also increase your voting power by contributing additional funds during the voting phase.
What happens if a startup doesn't deliver?
Funds are locked in a milestone-based escrow. If a startup fails to deliver proof for a milestone, the remaining funds stay in escrow and can be returned to investors through admin governance. This prevents rug pulls and protects investor capital.
Can I withdraw my investment during voting?
To maintain voting integrity, withdrawals during the voting phase incur a penalty. This prevents vote manipulation where investors could vote and then remove their backing. You can freely withdraw before voting begins or after distribution.
Which tokens are accepted?
Currently, pools accept USDC as the primary funding token. This ensures stable-value contributions and straightforward allocation math. Additional stablecoin support may be added through governance proposals.
